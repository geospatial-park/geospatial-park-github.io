<h2 class="legend-head">What I work on</h2>

<ul class="keys">
  <li>
    <b>Social sensing</b>
    <span>Turning geo-tagged social media into place-level signals — including a sentiment-based index for monitoring mental health at the neighborhood scale.</span>
  </li>
  <li>
    <b>Human mobility</b>
    <span>Measuring how people move between places using mobile phone and location data, and how those patterns shift under shocks like a pandemic.</span>
  </li>
  <li>
    <b>Network science</b>
    <span>Treating places as nodes and trips as edges, so centrality and connectivity become measurable properties of a neighborhood.</span>
  </li>
  <li>
    <b>Urban and environmental health</b>
    <span>Linking mobility and exposure — air quality, socioeconomic conditions — to health outcomes across U.S. metropolitan areas.</span>
  </li>
</ul>
