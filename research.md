<p class="eyebrow">Research</p>
<h1 class="page-title">Measuring places, movement, and health</h1>
<p class="page-intro">My research develops geospatial measures of urban life from large behavioural datasets — geo-tagged social media, mobile phone traces, census indicators, and environmental monitoring data. The recurring question is how a neighborhood's position in a city, and the movement running through it, shape the health of the people who live there.</p>
