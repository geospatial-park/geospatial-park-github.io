<p class="eyebrow">Teaching</p>
<h1 class="page-title">From first map to first script</h1>
<p class="page-intro">I teach across the GIS curriculum — foundational concepts, cartographic design, web mapping, and programming. My aim in each course is the same: students should leave able to work with real spatial data and defend the choices they made along the way.</p>
